HIIT vs. Zone 2: How to combine

Zone 2 builds mitochondrial density and aerobic base. HIIT improves VO₂max and glycolytic power. For most: 2× Zone 2 (30–45 min) + 1× HIIT (6–10 hard intervals) weekly alongside 2–3× strength training.

Protein timing & distribution

Total daily intake drives results most. Aim 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day. Distribute 20–40 g protein doses every 3–5 hours, with leucine‑rich sources (whey, eggs, lean meats, soy).

Sleep for hypertrophy & fat loss

Short sleep impairs recovery and hunger hormones. Target 7–9 h, consistent schedule, cool dark room, morning light, and reduce late caffeine. Naps can help if nights are short.

Mobility & pain‑free lifting

Use a warm‑up that targets joints through full range: controlled articular rotations, light sets, and tempo work. Train technique, not just load.

Intermittent fasting and training

IF can reduce calorie intake for some. Performance is individual; consider a protein‑forward first meal and place carbs around training.
